    About QTF

    Who is behind QTF?

    We are a group of queer activists, mental health providers, and Charlottesville community members! Our partners include our fiscal sponsor Fight Like A Grrrl (FLAG), Charlottesville Lady Arm Wrestlers (CLAW), Chinchilla Café, and others!

    What kind of projects and funding requests do you support?

    We support funding for one-on-one counseling sessions through a licensed therapist, group therapy sessions, wellness events focused on mental health, and other relevant projects.

    How do I apply for funding?

    Visit the 'Apply' section of our website and carefully read through the application materials. Applications are reviewed during biweekly sessions.

    How can I support the fund?

    You can support the fund via monetary donations at this link!

    If you are a licensed mental health provider and would like to get involved, please reach out via our contact form.
